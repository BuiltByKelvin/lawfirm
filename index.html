Our Background

Two Decades of Trusted Legal Practice

Brian O'Donnell is a dedicated attorney with over 24 years of experience providing effective legal solutions for individuals, businesses, and families across Hawaii and beyond. His practice is centered on real estate and property law, estate planning, trust and probate, business legal matters, contract review, and dispute resolution.

Board Certified in Estate Planning, Trust & Probate Law and a Fellow of the American College of Trust and Estate Counsel (ACTEC), Brian has built a reputation for meticulous work, honest counsel, and results that stand the test of time.

What sets Brian apart is not only his technical skill but the care he brings to every client relationship. He takes the time to understand each client's situation, explains the legal landscape in plain language, and builds a strategy tailored to their goals.

Whether negotiating a commercial contract, guiding a family through estate planning, or resolving a property dispute, Brian brings the same disciplined approach: listen carefully, prepare thoroughly, and advocate effectively.

Brian's practice is rooted in the belief that good legal work begins with understanding people — not just their cases. He works directly with every client, providing the personalized attention that a firm of any size should deliver. He measures success by the outcomes his clients experience long after the matter is closed.

Over the course of his career, Brian has represented buyers, sellers, landlords, investors, families, and small businesses across a broad range of legal matters — from complex real estate transactions to sensitive estate and probate issues. His work has helped families protect what they've built, helped businesses manage risk with clarity, and helped clients move forward with confidence.

He is admitted to practice in Hawaii and maintains an active presence in the state's legal community through ongoing involvement in professional organizations and continuing legal education. His office is located at Pauahi Tower at Bishop Square in the heart of Honolulu's business district.

Commercial Real Estate

Commercial Property Acquisition

Represented buyer in a $2M commercial property purchase, coordinating title review, due diligence, and closing.

Details: Brian conducted extensive title research dating back 30 years, negotiated with multiple lienholders, and ensured all zoning and environmental compliance requirements were met before finalizing the acquisition.

Title Dispute

Homeowner Title Dispute

Represented a homeowner facing a boundary and title claim affecting property rights and resale value.

Details: Successfully argued that the adverse possession claim was invalid due to a break in the chain of continuous possession. The court ruled in favor of our client, preserving their full ownership and property value.

Estate Planning

Comprehensive Estate Plan

Designed a trust and estate plan for a family with multiple properties and business interests.

Details: Created a revocable living trust, established LLC structures for property management, and implemented strategies to minimize estate taxes and avoid probate for the surviving spouse and children.

Business Legal

Contract Negotiation

Reviewed and renegotiated a commercial contract for a small business client, addressing risk exposure.

Details: Identified highly unfavorable indemnification clauses in the original agreement. Negotiated new terms that limited liability, protected intellectual property, and provided clear dispute resolution mechanisms.
